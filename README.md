# Simple Interest Calculator

## Overview

This project describes a simple interest calculator. It calculates the interest earned or paid on an initial amount (principal) over a specified period at a fixed interest rate.

## Formula

Simple interest is calculated using:

```text
interest = principal × annual_rate × time / 100
```

Where:

- **principal**: initial amount of money.
- **annual_rate**: annual interest rate, expressed as a percentage.
- **time**: duration in years.

The total amount at the end of the period is:

```text
total_amount = principal + interest
```

## Example

For a principal of 1,000.00, an annual interest rate of 5%, and a period of 2 years:

```text
interest = 1000 × 5 × 2 / 100 = 100
 total_amount = 1000 + 100 = 1100
```

The simple interest is **100.00**, and the final amount is **1,100.00** in the same currency as the principal.

## Inputs

- Initial principal amount
- Annual interest rate (percentage)
- Time period (years)

## Output

- Simple interest amount
- Total amount (principal plus interest)

## Notes

- This calculator assumes a fixed annual interest rate.
- It uses simple interest, so interest does not accrue on previously accumulated interest.
- The example is illustrative and does not account for fees, taxes, or compounding.
