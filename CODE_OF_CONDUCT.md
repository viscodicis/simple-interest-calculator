# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, are committed to making participation in this project respectful and free from harassment. Everyone should be able to participate regardless of their background, identity, experience, appearance, or personal circumstances. We will work to maintain a welcoming, inclusive, and constructive community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that supports a positive community include:

- Communicating respectfully and showing consideration for others.
- Listening to different perspectives and experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback professionally.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Acting in the interest of the community as a whole.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualized language, unwanted sexual attention, sharing someone else's private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for explaining these standards and responding fairly to reported or observed misconduct. They may edit or remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and should explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and project events. It also applies when a person publicly represents the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through the project's issue or contact process. Reports should be reviewed promptly and fairly. Anyone handling a report must respect the reporter's privacy and the security of everyone involved.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider the seriousness, context, and impact of a violation when choosing a response. Possible responses include:

### 1. Correction

For a minor or first-time issue, a maintainer may privately explain why the behavior was inappropriate and ask the person to correct it.

### 2. Warning

For a more serious incident or repeated behavior, a formal warning may be issued with clear expectations and limits on further interaction.

### 3. Temporary Ban

For serious or persistent violations, temporary restrictions may be placed on participation in project spaces and communications.

### 4. Permanent Ban

For a sustained pattern of violations, harassment, threats, or other serious misconduct, a person may be permanently excluded from project spaces.
